Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my visa status?

Simply enter your Application ID, Passport Number, Date of Birth, and Visa Type in the form above. Click "Check Status" to view your application status instantly.

What is my Application ID?

Your Application ID is provided in the confirmation email you received after submitting your visa application. It starts with "MALDOVA-"

How long does it take to process a visa?

Processing times vary by visa type: Tourist (5-7 days), Student (10-15 days), Work (15-30 days), Business (7-10 days)

Can I track my application status?

Yes! You can check your status anytime using this portal. Simply enter your details and you'll see the latest status of your application.

What if my status shows "Rejected"?

If your application is rejected, you'll see the reason in the status details. You can contact our support team or reapply after addressing the issues.

Is this website secure?

Yes, this is the official Maldova Visa Portal. All your data is encrypted and secure. Never share your details on unofficial websites.
